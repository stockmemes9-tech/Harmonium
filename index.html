🪗 Lid Harmonium

Move your laptop lid and the harmonium plays. Tilt up to climb the scale, tilt down to descend. Move faster for louder.

Press Start. The browser will ask for camera permission (used only to sense the lid tilting; nothing is recorded or uploaded).

Manual control (also works if no sensor or camera is available): tap or drag across the keys, use ← → keys, or this slider.

Tip: sit in front of the laptop with a normally lit, steady background (a wall or room behind you works well). Walking around behind you can confuse the camera detection.
